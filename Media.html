Transformed by Travels Launches AI-Powered Platform That Matches Travelers to Destinations and Experiences Based on Their Travel Personality

Free access available through August 2026 with promo code SUMMER_FREE.

Minneapolis, August 7, 2026 — Transformed by Travels today announced the launch of its AI-powered travel planning platform, designed to help travelers design intentional journeys that align with who they are — and who they want to become.

Transformational travel is one of the fastest-growing segments in the industry. Today's travelers increasingly want to return home with more than just photos — they want to come back as a better version of themselves. Hotels, destinations, and tour operators are beginning to respond, but most travel planning tools still treat every traveler the same way. Transformed by Travels takes a different approach.

"We believe travel is one of the most powerful catalysts for personal growth. Our platform doesn't just help you plan a trip — it helps you understand who you are as a traveler and design a meaningful travel experience that actually moves you forward." — Michael Emerson, founder, Transformed by Travels

How It Works

The platform delivers in three steps:

  1. Discover Your Travel Personality. A free Travel Style Assessment — the only travel personality assessment designed specifically for personal growth — reveals your unique traveler archetype and what genuinely drives fulfillment in your life.
  2. Connect Travel to Who You Want to Become. Learn how intentional travel can accelerate personal development, with a framework built around your specific goals and aspirations.
  3. Plan Smarter with AI-Powered Tools. Use a personalized destination matching platform to explore the world through the lens of your unique personality.

The difference shows up immediately in how recommendations are made. A Cultural Explorer — someone drawn to history, art, and deep human connection — receives destination suggestions like Athens, Kyoto, and Istanbul rather than a generic resort stay. An Adventure-Oriented Traveler sees options like Patagonia and Glacier National Park. Every recommendation is grounded in travel self-discovery, not algorithms built for the average tourist.

Built on a Global Destination Database

The platform launches with a database of over 3,000 destinations worldwide, each scored for compatibility with different traveler personalities. Combined with AI-powered trip planning tools, daily travel journaling, and post-trip integration support, Transformed by Travels offers an end-to-end experience unlike anything else in the market. Every visitor to transformedbytravels.com can take the Travel Style Assessment for free and gain immediate insight into their travel personality.

Co-Travel Compatibility

Transformed by Travels provides special value for traveling couples and small groups who often struggle to reconcile divergent travel personalities. The platform surfaces travel compatibility insights — identifying potential stress points and recommending destinations and experiences that work for both personalities — making it a powerful tool for meaningful travel together.
