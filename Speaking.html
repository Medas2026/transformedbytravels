Speaking

Michael Emerson

Global speaker · Adventure traveler · Technology visionary

Michael Emerson is a global speaker, adventure traveler, and technology visionary. He reveals to audiences the potential of travel to help them reach their personal goals. Drawing on a lifetime of exploration and years of work in psychology and technology, he shows how travel can become one of the most powerful tools we have for growth and transformation.

Michael has spoken at more than 100 corporate events around the world, addressing audiences ranging from intimate leadership groups of 10 to conference halls of 7,500 people. His speaking experience spans keynote presentations, hosted events, and on-stage interviews with distinguished guests and fellow speakers. He has appeared as a weekly expert on TV, and his ideas have been featured in magazines, television news programs, and industry newsletters.

A PhD candidate in Psychology with an emphasis on research and assessment, Michael combines academic insight with real-world experience. He has spent more than a decade working in assessment-driven coaching, helping individuals better understand themselves and reach their potential. His research focuses on transformational travel — how meaningful experiences away from home can reshape our perspectives, values, and identity.

What makes Michael's message especially compelling is that he lives the adventure he describes. He had to overcome hydrophobia to become a world-class scuba diver. He has survived typhoons at sea and a harrowing medical evacuation from a remote island — experiences that give his insights unusual force. As President of Transformed by Travels, he also brings a leadership perspective that resonates strongly with corporate audiences.

Through storytelling, original research, and touches of humor and irony, Michael inspires audiences to see travel — and life itself — as a journey of discovery, growth, and transformation. He leaves audiences with an entirely new perspective on how travel can change their lives for the better.
