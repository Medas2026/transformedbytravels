What Stawi reaches
Reservation → arrival · weeks to months
Before
TTC: pre-trip preparation
  • Traveler Style Assessment — one of five segments, and how they like to be guided
  • "I want to go from ___ to ___" — revisited each month until it is theirs
  • Monthly journaling, so the aspiration can surface before they travel
  • Couples see each other — the compatibility report lands before the trip, not after
The stay
During
TTC: experience & reflection
  • A daily companion — conditions, sun and moon, what is on
  • Guidance matched to style and aspiration
  • Journaling and a photo or two a day
  • The staff know how to host them — before they arrive, not by day three
Check-out → six months · where it holds or dissolves
After
TTC: evaluation & integration
  • Week two — their own words and photographs returned. What has changed?
  • Months two to five — one short prompt a month. Deliberately sparse
  • Month six — the assessment retaken, the movement shown, the whole arc as a keepsake
  • One well-earned invitation back, at the moment it means something
  • Evidence for the property — which aspirations hold, and what produced them
